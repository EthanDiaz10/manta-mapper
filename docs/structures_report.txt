  rib boss bearing: 0.37 of allowable (Tip strike: fin drags back and down (30 N))
  sleeve-to-boss epoxy bond: 0.59 of allowable (Tip strike: fin drags back and down (30 N))
  (repeated cases count against half the printed/bond strength)
  anti-rotation pin 3 mm at 35% root chord behind the spar: bearing 0.13 of allowable (Gust +7.6 m/s at V_C 20 m/s, 1.18 kg)
Carry-through at x = 205 mm (sleeve inner ends at y = +-74 mm): 1 x 10x1 mm carbon strip in the floor and 1 in the roof, 36 mm apart
  ultimate cap force 801 N; bond each strip end >= 80 mm to the rib
Panel retention: launch pulls each panel out along the swept spar with 6.2 N (limit); hold it with >= 19 N and let go above ~3x that so a cartwheel pops the panel off instead of breaking the sleeve
